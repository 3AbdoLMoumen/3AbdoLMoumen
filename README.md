# Assalam Alikoum, I'm Iyad AbdeL'Moumen

Medical student and machine learning researcher working at the intersection
of deep learning and healthcare.

I focus on building complete, well-understood systems — custom architectures,
layers, and training loops rather than black-box fine-tuning. Alongside applied
work, I am progressively studying the mathematical and theoretical foundations
of deep learning: optimization, inductive biases, and the formal reasoning
behind why architectures work.

**NVIDIA GTC presenter** (2 posters, one featured in keynote) · Conference speaker ·
Active researcher in medical imaging and multimodal AI

---

## Research & Technical Interests

- **Medical Imaging** — semantic segmentation, classification, and detection
  across MRI, fundus imaging, OCT, and ultrasound
- **Bio Medical Signals** — ECGs, EEGs, Classification Processing
- **Architecture Design** — custom layers, attention mechanisms,
  encoder-decoder networks, metric learning, GANs
- **Multimodal AI** — vision-language models, medical report generation,
  cross-modal representation learning
- **Computer Vision** — object detection, image recognition,
  geometric matching, feature learning, custom projects for real world usage (plate detection, face recognitions, surveillence, PPE detection)
- **Deep Learning Theory** *(ongoing study)* — mathematical foundations,
  inductive biases, optimization dynamics, loss landscape geometry
